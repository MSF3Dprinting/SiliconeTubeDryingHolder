# Lessons learned — Silicone tube drying holder (v1.0–v2.0, 2026-09-26/27)

- **Rounded edges on a non-convex outline without minkowski:** sweep one convex 2D profile along the outline — `linear_extrude` for straight runs, `rotate_extrude(angle)` for the fillet arcs (profile mirrored at left turns, plain at right turns), a flat core inset into the band's flat land. Fast in Manifold (1.4 s) and in the 2021.01 OpenCSG preview; the same `sweep_band()` makes the cove ring round the shelf root. Worth moving into helpers.scad.
- **Profile width must be exact at convex arcs:** a `hull()` of circles is wider than its square (2 × r); pass the width to the profile through a `$` variable and build the profile as an explicit `polygon()` — a 2D `intersection()` inside `rotate_extrude()` produced an 858-body mesh in Manifold.
- **Manifold accepts what 2021.01 refuses:** a rotate_extrude profile crossing the axis exported a "clean" STL through openscad-wasm but errored in the native 2021.01 preview; `render_views.py` reports "ok" whenever a PNG exists, so grep the native log for `ERROR` before trusting the sheet.
- **Adapt instead of refusing:** the screw spacing is clamped to the plate width (with an `echo` NOTE) so a one-slot comb renders at the default spacing; a sweep with 13 guards and 0 FAILs is a pass, but every guard message must say what to change.
- **Wedge holds have no clearance to coupon:** a single-slot `part = "test_slot"` variant is the coupon; document the deviation.
- **A friction wedge is not a hold:** the requester rejected the v1.0 taper because a tube that is not pushed in hard falls; the fix was to turn the taper 90° — a V through the shelf thickness (rope-cleat principle) holds by geometry, prints as vertical walls and needs the same sweep code with a different profile and the bottom-face outline. Ask "what holds it if nobody pushes?" before choosing a friction fit for a hanging item.
- **One profile, one exception:** sweeping the V profile around the whole outline puts an inclined face on the buried back edge too, which came out of the plate as a 60° overhang; a per-run flag (`$band_back`) gives that run a plain rectangle. Check every run of a sweep against what it is buried in.
- **A buried feature passes every check:** the v1.0 cove ring was rotated into the plate (rotate([90,0,0]) sends the profile depth to −Y), so it never existed — and check_stl, the sweep and the renders were all green. Confirm a feature exists with a section (trimesh section at 1 mm in front of the plate: the cove widens the shelf) before writing it into the README.
- **Zero-area specks at tangent overlaps:** with a filleted profile, the 0.01 mm overlap between a swept run and its arc puts two tangent surfaces (a fillet chord and its revolved copy) through each other; Manifold then leaves 2-face, zero-area bodies at a few parameter values (main body still watertight). Removing the overlap is worse (coincident faces, more specks). Fix used: a cleanup step in the exporter that drops detached bodies below 0.01 mm² and reports it; the checks still run on the cleaned file.
- **Detached sweeps die here:** background `sweep.py` runs were killed twice mid-way in this sandbox; run the sweep in foreground chunks (`--params`) and merge the reports.
- **Soft tubes bend, they do not compress:** a thin-walled silicone tube gives ~1 mm under 1 N on a 12 mm contact (ring bending, δ ≈ 0.15·F·R³/EI), so every hold that presses on the outside of a soft tube either squeezes it visibly (rigid V, wedge) or holds it weakly (bounded pocket). Say this to the requester with numbers before the third iteration, not after.
- **Ask what may touch the tube:** "nothing inside the lumen" only came out after the cone proposal. For CSSD items, ask at intake which surfaces of the item may be touched and by what.
- **Stair slots need a fillet budget:** a 1 mm step cannot carry two 0.8 mm fillets — the fillet polygon self-intersects and Manifold silently returns a non-watertight mesh (0 boundary edges, inverted faces). Guard 2·r ≤ step length before generating the polygon.
- **`linear_extrude(scale=…)` slices:** with $fn = 96 a scaled extrusion is cut into ~100 slices (260 k faces for one core). For a pure scale the side faces are planar: set `slices = 1`.
- **Swept bands do not fit small convex plan corners:** the band needs r ≥ band width + 0.5 at every convex arc, so a stepped outline (0.4 mm shoulder tips) is built as a drafted core plus inset 2D slices per face for the edge rounding — one mechanism rounds outer edges and slot edges alike.
